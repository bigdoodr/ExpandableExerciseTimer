# Expandable Exercise Timer
A flexible xOS workout timer app that lets you create custom exercise routines with timed or rep-based exercises, configurable rest periods, and audio alerts.
Features

- Custom Exercise Routines - Add, remove, and rearrange exercises to build your perfect workout
- Flexible Exercise Types - Set each exercise as time-based or rep-based
- Configurable Rest Periods - Specify how long to rest after each exercise
- Timer Controls - Pause, resume, or cancel your routine at any time
- Audio Alerts - Get notified with sound when each timer expires
- Screen Wake - Keep your device screen active during workouts
- Import/Export - Save and share your workout routines
- Completion Notifications - Receive a notification when your routine finishes

## Use Cases

- HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training)
- Circuit training
- Strength training routines
- Tabata workouts
- CrossFit WODs
- Custom fitness routines

## Requirements

- iOS/iPadOS 18.6 or later
- macOS 15.6 or later
- visionOS 26.2 or later

## Privacy
Expandable Exercise Timer does not transmit any data to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

**Health data (HealthKit).** With your permission, the app reads your heart rate, active energy burned, and date of birth (used only to calculate your maximum heart rate for heart-rate-zone display) during a workout. When a workout finishes, it writes that workout session to the Health app. This data is read from and written to Apple Health only — it never leaves your device or your iCloud account, and the developer has no access to it. You can revoke this access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

**Workout routines.** Your custom exercise routines are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage, and are only included in iCloud/device backups you've configured yourself. They are not sent to the developer or any third party.

**Watch connectivity.** Data exchanged between the iPhone and Watch apps (timer state, heart rate readings) stays on your paired devices via Apple's WatchConnectivity framework and is never transmitted externally.
